Carhartt WIP, 2026

Category Builder

Problem. Seasonal categorization ran on an Excel macro that guessed product types from names. Plain tees ended up under Graphic Prints, and every run needed manual clean-up.

Built. An AI-assisted tool that derives every category from PIM, checks each one against the live category tree, and only removes a category when one attribute proves it wrong. Sale campaigns land in both the campaign and the regular sale tree automatically. Weekly hand-picked categorization is now automatic, and I rolled it out with AI tool training for the team.

In daily use by three team members

Campaign Product Picker

Problem. Campaign edits were picked by hand in spreadsheets, and dates broke on import.

Built. A workflow that proposes the edit, rules out sale and recently featured items, and produces import files with dates intact.
