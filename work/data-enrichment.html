Automating product data enrichment

Search and filters only work with what the catalog describes, and our enrichment was slow and inconsistent from season to season. I audited it, cut it back, then automated the attributes discovery depends on.

The problem

Discovery depended on product data that was slow to produce and inconsistent to use.

Rules that fought each other

313 enrichment rules had grown one at a time, and category logic overlapped and conflicted.

Outfits linked by hand

Complete-the-look links and model records were entered manually every season.

One attribute, many meanings

Color and fabric were written separately per language, so filters behaved differently by market.

What I did

313 rules, audited

The PIM had grown one rule at a time. We reviewed all 313, consolidated the category rules and rewrote the ones that conflicted.

313 rules, each reviewed

Attributes from the product itself

AI rules now set color and fabric, written in English first and translated after, so every market gets the same meaning.

The call

Results

Enrichment now runs on a smaller rule set, and the manual outfit work is gone.

What worked

  • Consolidating before automating, so every AI rule sits on a clean base.
  • Writing attributes in English first gave every market the same meaning.

What didn't

  • We didn't measure attribute coverage at the start, so the impact shows as counts rather than coverage gained.

What I'd do differently

I'd roll automation out category by category instead of season by season, so each gain is visible sooner and easier to attribute.
