Privé: an invitation-only VIP shop

Mytheresa wanted a shop open only to its top customers, with exclusive designer drops. I took it from a gated US pilot to a global rollout, then handed it to Operations.

The problem

A shop for selected top customers had to feel exclusive, and still convert.

Exclusive by design

Access had to be limited to selected top customers, which ruled out a normal shop launch.

Designers to convince

The concept only worked if designer brands committed to exclusive drops.

No playbook

Nothing like it existed at Mytheresa, so the first version had to be built from scratch.

What I did

Defined the goals and started small

I set the goals with the C-suite and launched a US pilot with access gated by customer segment, and ordering through personal shoppers.

The call

  • Launch globally with full checkout

    Fastest reach, but every assumption untested at once.

  • What we did

    Gated US pilot, then expand on data

    Small, controlled, and the pilot data decided what came next.

Results

Conversion rose six times from the US pilot to the global rollout.

6xconversion, 0.2% to 1.2%

What worked

  • Starting small kept the risk low and produced the evidence for direct checkout.
  • Training Operations meant future launches no longer depended on product.

What didn't

  • Ordering through personal shoppers didn't scale, so it had to be replaced by direct checkout.

What I'd do differently

I'd define the expansion criteria before the pilot starts, so moving to direct checkout is a planned gate rather than a later decision.
